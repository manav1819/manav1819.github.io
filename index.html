About Me

I hold an Honours Bachelor of Information Sciences in Cyber Security from Sheridan College, where I was technical lead on a capstone security-awareness platform — directing backend architecture and access control through to production deployment. Since February 2023 I've worked full-time as a Concierge / Security Officer at a Digital Realty data center in Vaughan, monitoring access control and CCTV for a facility serving four client tenants and routing incidents through ServiceNow. That ground-level experience with access control, monitoring, and escalation is what I now practise on the digital side in my home SOC lab.

My work spans both sides of the fence: defensive practices like detection engineering, log analysis, and threat modeling, and offensive fundamentals like exploit development and CTF competitions, because understanding how systems are broken is what makes defending them meaningful. Cybersecurity is an ever-evolving field — new vulnerabilities, new frameworks, new attack surfaces — and I treat continuous learning as a core part of the job, not a phase that ends at graduation.

TraceLite

Personal / Open Source

An open-source Bitcoin fund-flow tracer, built after losing funds to a scam and wanting the tooling to exist for other small-scale scam victims. Deliberately non-attributional — it tracks where funds moved, not who owns the wallets. Written in Python with NetworkX, it performs depth-limited traversal (3, 5, or 10 hops) against the Blockstream and Mempool.space APIs, applying fan-in/fan-out and rapid-forwarding heuristics so victims can visualize where stolen funds went.
